I accept private pay and most branches of Delaware Medicaid.

I do not charge a higher rate for the initial intake session.

Individual Counseling for Adults: $175 per session

Relationship Counseling: $250 per session

Family Therapy: $200 per session

For individuals and families paying out of pocket, I will provide you with the correct receipts (called superbills) so that you can submit them to your insurance
company.

After consultation and before we begin treatment, I will provide you with the correct CPT code. I will ask you to call the number on the back of your insurance card and ask if you have out-of-network mental health benefits and a deductible. Generally, PPO and POS plans reimburse 60-80% of the cost once your deductible is met, while HMO usually do not offer any out-of-network benefits.

If you do not qualify for out-of-network benefits and my rate is financially unsustainable for you, I may be able to offer reduced rates based on your income. If I am unable to provide you with a reduced rate, I will support you in the process of finding another trusted provider.
