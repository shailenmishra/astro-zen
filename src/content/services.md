**Free Consultation**

I offer a free 15-minute consultation to see if we would be a good fit.

**Individual Counseling for Adults - 50 minutes / weekly / 8-20 weeks**

Sessions will help you build a secure connection with yourself, manage difficult emotions, and heal from past trauma. This will help guide you to transform negative self-criticism, reduce anxiety and depression, and form healthier relationships with others. Psycho-social tools and resources will be provided in our work together.

Topics may include: pre-conception, infertility, loss, postpartum, emotional dysregulation, intrusive thoughts, feeling lost in parenthood, anxiety, depression, and other mood disorders.

**Relationship Counseling - 50-60 minutes / weekly / 12 weeks**

As a couple you may find that you want to work on your relationship pre-conception, during pregnancy, or as you adapt to raising young children. I offer couples therapy to build a united front, practice communication strategies, repair emotional distress, stop negative fight cycles, and build a safe, long-lasting bond with your partner.

**Family Therapy for children 3-10 years old - 60-120 minutes / weekly or biweekly / 4-12 weeks**

Our initial meeting will involve all available family members (the child, parents, and siblings) to address immediate issues as well as behaviors that brought the family to therapy. This will be a time for me, your therapist, to observe the child’s social, emotional, and cognitive skills through natural play with their parents and siblings inside the comfort of their home.

**Whole-Family Sessions:** The focus is on building secure emotional bonds, improving communication, and resolving conflict within the whole family. It helps remove blame from one child and includes everyone in the healing process.

**Parent(s) and Child:** establish secure attachment, repair emotional bonds, stop negative interaction patterns, and reframe a child’s behavior through play-based observation and support.

**Parent(s)-Only Sessions:** This removes pressure off the child while continuing to center treatment around their needs. This approach allows parents to address their own dysregulation, learn to co-regulate with their child, and process feelings of guilt and burnout that would not be possible or appropriate with their child in session.

Along with the DSM (Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders) I use the DC:0–5™ Diagnostic Classification of Mental Health and Developmental Disorders of Infancy and Early Childhood.
