I’m Colleen–a licensed clinical social worker, therapist, mother, and spouse.

My aim is for families to cultivate deeper connections, simplify, and slow down in a rushed and ever-changing world. I support hopeful parents and current parents in finding peace in their inner self, so they can approach parenting with more steadiness.

I provide psychotherapy for individual adults, couples, and parents of young children who are navigating pre-conception through the beginning of adolescence. You might hear me use the term “The Young Child” which is more typically used in early education and policy spaces but here’s why I’m using it in my practice: The Young Child is a comprehensive term, including pre-birth through the beginning of adolescence. From years of working with families, navigating my own struggles with infertility, and now a parent of two young children, I know these early years as families are beautiful and messy.

My purpose is to support you and your family in your journey so that you can live a more cohesive life. I am an emotionally focused therapist which means I help facilitate emotional processing that assists in the creation of more secure relationships.

I approach therapy through these lenses: mindfulness-based, culturally responsive, trauma-informed, and attachment-focused. All sessions are conducted virtually over a HIPAA compliant telehealth service.
